A LITTLE ABOUT US

Art belongs
to everyone.

Colorcraft turns the moments you’ve already captured into something you can make with your own hands.

A creative pause

Colorcraft is a simple photo-to-paint-by-numbers maker. Choose a photo, decide how much detail you’d like, and the generator turns its colors and shapes into a numbered outline and matching palette.

Made for your kind of making

Choose fewer colors for bigger, calmer painting areas, or more colors for finer detail. Your generated kit includes a color preview, a printable numbered outline, a scalable SVG, and a numbered color palette.

A note about the generator

Every photo is different, and generated patterns are an artistic interpretation rather than an exact reproduction. For the most paintable result, try a clear photo with one main subject and good light.
