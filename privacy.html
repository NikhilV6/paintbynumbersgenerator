THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Your photos are
yours.

A plain-language note about what happens when you make a template.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This notice describes what the application code does. Before this app is made public, its operator must add their identity, a privacy contact, hosting provider details, and any applicable retention periods, and confirm that this notice matches the deployed configuration and local legal requirements.

The photo you choose

When you select “Create my template,” your browser sends the selected photo to the configured backend so it can generate your preview, outline, palette, and PDF. The application processes the photo for that request, does not save it as a persistent app record, and removes its temporary generated files when processing finishes. The backend host necessarily receives the upload and may process it in server memory or temporary storage.

The hosting provider may make its own logs, backups, or operational records. The app cannot control what the operator or hosting provider retains. Only upload pictures you have permission to use and are comfortable sending to the server that operates this site.

Information and browser storage

Colorcraft does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, or payment information. The browser version does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your chosen photo preview is held in the current browser tab so you can review it; it is not saved by this page to an account or app database.

The web server or hosting provider may keep technical access logs, such as IP address, request time, and requested URL, according to its configuration. The operator should document the actual log data and retention period here before launch.

Third-party services

The app code does not send your photo to an analytics or image-hosting provider. The operator should list any hosting, monitoring, analytics, or other third-party services used by the live site here before launch.

Your choices

You can use the app without selecting an image. To discard the current preview, close or refresh the browser tab. For questions about a hosted version, contact the person or organization operating that version.
